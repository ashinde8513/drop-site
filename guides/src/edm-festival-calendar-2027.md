## How do I stay on top of date changes?

Festivals move dates, add weekends and swap venues. Three habits that help:

1. Treat any date here as a plan, not a booking. Check the festival site before you buy flights or a hotel.
2. Sign up for the festival's own newsletter. Announcements usually hit email first.
3. Don't trust a date until it's on the festival's own page.
