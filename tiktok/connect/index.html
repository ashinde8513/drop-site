Connect TikTok to Drop

This connects Drop's own TikTok account (@trydrop.app) so Drop can post its own concert clips. It does not read or post to anyone else's account.

Drop asks TikTok for three permissions: basic profile info (user.info.basic), upload videos (video.upload) and publish videos (video.publish). You approve them on TikTok. Drop never sees your TikTok password.
