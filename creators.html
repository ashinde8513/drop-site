Colorado founding pilot · 10–15 creators

Help shape how Colorado finds shows.

Drop is assembling a six-week founding cohort of Denver and Boulder EDM creators, photographers, artists, student tastemakers, and show-focused accounts. There is no follower minimum. We care more about relevance, original work, trust, and follow-through.

  • ✓Early beta access and a Drop Creator profile badge
  • ✓Lifetime Drop+ and a readable creator code
  • ✓Limited campaign-specific ticket opportunities when inventory is secured
  • ✓Your honest voice—no scripted praise or required positive review

Tickets are not guaranteed and may never be available for a particular show. Paid campaigns and revenue-sharing opportunities may be added as Drop grows; no payment is guaranteed.

Apply to the founding cohort

Applicants must be 18+, based in Colorado, and able to join one feedback session and publish two original Drop-related pieces during the pilot.

What participation means

  • One beta-feedback session
  • Two original Drop-related pieces
  • Clear disclosure on every benefit-connected Drop mention
  • Honest opinions remain yours

How creators are selected

Local and audience relevance and content quality each carry 30% of the score, authentic engagement 20%, reliability and brand fit 15%, and reach 5%. The acceptance threshold is 70%.

Disclosure and tickets

Say plainly when Drop gave you a benefit or ticket. A hashtag alone is not enough. Any ticket is campaign-specific, non-resalable, subject to venue rules, and never guaranteed.
