    Weekend before Thanksgiving week. Plenty on, then a quiet stretch. Here is what I would plan around.

    The Black Box 10 Year Anniversary · The Black Box · Fri Nov 20 and Sat Nov 21, 9:00pm

    Two nights for a room that has been part of the scene for a decade. Check the venue page for each night's details.

    Level Up, Modal Notes, Blossom, Mythm, Ancestyr · Mission Ballroom · Fri Nov 20, 9:00pm

    Five acts on one bill. Bass-leaning.

    Tvboo, Doctor P, Twopercent b2b Ozztin, Chozen, Gallium · Mission Ballroom · Sat Nov 21, 8:00pm
