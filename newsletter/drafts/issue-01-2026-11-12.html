    Drop picks · Issue 1 · Thu Nov 12, 2026

    Draft, not published. Not sent and not live until Arya approves it.

    Hey. Founder here.

    Big week for the front range. Red Rocks is open in November, which still feels like cheating. Here are the nights I would actually go to.

    Gryffin, Oliver Heldens, Ar/Co · Red Rocks, Morrison · Fri Nov 13, 6:00pm

    The main one this week. It is cold up there in November, so dress for it. Check the venue page for bag and seating rules.

    TOKiMONSTA · The Church Nightclub · Fri Nov 13, 10:00pm

    For the late-night crowd who want a club room after the amphitheater crowd goes home.

    Desert Dwellers · Cervantes', Ballroom · Fri Nov 13, 7:30pm

    Downtempo and bass, in a room built for it. Early start, so it works if you have work Saturday.

    Smoakland · Mission Ballroom · Sat Nov 14, 9:00pm

    Big-room Saturday. Check the Mission page for age and doors.

    Fox Stevenson & Mystery Skulls · Summit Music Hall · Thu Nov 12, 7:00pm

    Thursday option for drum and bass and synth-pop fans.

    One useful thing: Red Rocks is a climb. Leave the heels at home, and book your ride home before the show starts.

    From us: we built Drop because we kept missing shows we would have gone to. If something should be on this list and is not, reply and tell me.
