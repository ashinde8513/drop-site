# About Drop

Drop is a social website, iPhone app, and Android app for discovering live music, with a focus on EDM and electronic shows: raves, festivals, club nights, and concerts. Listings link out to the official ticketing page when the seller provides one.

Drop puts real events from real venues and promoters in one place, plus the social layer that tells you who's already going.

- **Built by:** Resonance Ventures LLC, in Colorado.
- **Coverage:** starts in Denver, Colorado — see [City guides](city-guides.md).
- **Not a ticket seller:** Drop is not a ticket seller, box office, promoter, or event organizer. Ticket purchases happen on the official seller's site ([Terms](https://trydropapp.com/terms)).
- **Price:** free to browse — see [Get the app](app.md) and [FAQ](faq.md).
